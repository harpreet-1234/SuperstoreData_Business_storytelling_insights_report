# Superstore Sales — Data Storytelling and Business Insights

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Superstore Sales dataset to identify important business patterns and convert them into meaningful business insights and recommendations.

## Objective

* Analyze sales performance across products, regions, and customer segments.
* Identify high- and low-performing areas.
* Understand order patterns and sales trends over time.
* Provide business recommendations based on the findings.

## Tools Used

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook

## Key Insights

* Technology generated the highest total sales among the major product categories.
* Phones generated the highest sales among the product sub-categories.
* West was the highest-performing region, while South recorded the lowest sales.
* Consumer customers generated the highest total revenue.
* Office Supplies had the highest number of unique orders despite having lower total revenue.
* Sales showed an overall increasing trend from 2015 to 2018, with 2018 recording the highest sales.

## Conclusion

The analysis highlights the strongest revenue-generating categories, customer segments, regions, order patterns, and sales trends. These insights can support data-driven decisions and help identify opportunities for business growth and improvement.
